Currency strength

Equal-weight one-day performance against the other seven currencies.

Source: Frankfurter daily reference rates. No intraday ticks. The effective dates and sample size are shown after loading. Rates are for comparison, not execution.

Loading daily reference data…

Calculation methodology

Currency strength is the mean percentage change versus the other seven currencies from the previous available observation. Pair changes use the last two available observations. Volatility is the sample standard deviation of up to 30 daily simple returns, expressed as a daily percentage; it is not ATR or annualized volatility. Weekends and missing publication days are omitted. Cross rates are derived from a shared USD base.
