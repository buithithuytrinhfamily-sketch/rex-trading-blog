Examples are hypothetical. Verify contract specifications, costs and conversion rates with your provider. Planned stop losses are not guaranteed execution prices.

How this calculation works

Reward multiple = absolute(target − entry) / absolute(entry − stop). This is a price-distance ratio before costs.

Entry 100, stop 95 and target 110 gives 2 units of planned reward for 1 unit of planned risk. A high ratio alone says nothing about the probability of reaching the target.
