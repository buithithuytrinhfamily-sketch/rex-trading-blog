How this calculation works

This is a manual breadth score, not a live sentiment feed or the BabyPips meter. Stocks rising, volatility falling and credit spreads narrowing each contribute +1. Opposite moves contribute −1; unchanged contributes 0.

Score = sum / 3 × 100. Equal weights and directional signs are a deliberately simple worksheet. Divergent signals are common and the score does not recommend a trade.
