Risk disclosure

Understand what the educational content and planned-risk calculations cannot promise.

Trading gold, forex, leveraged products and cryptoassets can result in substantial losses. Some products or arrangements can expose you to losses beyond an initial margin deposit. Protections depend on the product, provider and jurisdiction.

Stops may execute at different prices during gaps, fast markets or limited liquidity. A calculated risk budget is a plan, not a guarantee. Past performance and individual testimonials do not establish future results.

Rex does not manage your account through these pages. The existing channel is supported by a broker-paid introducing commission as disclosed on the homepage. Consider costs, counterparty risk and whether you understand the product before making any decision.
