Read a candle in context

A candle summarizes a period; it does not explain the cause of a move.

A standard candle records open, high, low and close over a chosen interval. The body spans open to close; the wicks show extremes. The same trade stream creates different patterns on five-minute and daily charts because aggregation changes what you see.

A long wick only says price visited an area and closed elsewhere. It does not prove manipulation, buying pressure or the next direction. Start with a defined timeframe and objective rules for a pattern, then measure outcomes across enough examples, including losing ones.

Worked example

A candle opens at 100, reaches 104, falls to 99 and closes at 103. Its body runs from 100 to 103; the full range is five points. These values do not reveal the order of every intraperiod move.
