The three rules

Three rules I never break.

01

Every signal has a stop loss

Before you think about profit, you decide what you’re willing to lose. Capital is oxygen, you protect it first.

02

I post my losses

The losers go on the board next to the winners. A channel that only shows wins is selling a feeling, not a method.

03

I never promise profit

Nobody honestly can. I promise the reasoning behind each trade and a stop loss on every one. That’s the whole deal.
